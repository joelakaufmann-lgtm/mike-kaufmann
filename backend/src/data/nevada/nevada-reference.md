# Nevada Law Reference (Curated Outline)

This is a curated, high-level outline of Nevada legal authorities and conventions used by Mike Kaufmann to keep AI responses grounded in Nevada law. It is NOT legal advice and is not a substitute for primary research in the Nevada Revised Statutes (NRS), Nevada Administrative Code (NAC), Nevada Rules of Court, or controlling case law.

## 1. Court Structure

- **Nevada Supreme Court** — court of last resort; mandatory jurisdiction over appeals not assigned to the Court of Appeals.
- **Nevada Court of Appeals** — intermediate appellate court; hears matters deflected by the Supreme Court under NRAP 17.
- **District Courts** — general-jurisdiction trial courts organized by judicial district (e.g., Eighth Judicial District = Clark County; Second Judicial District = Washoe County).
- **Justice Courts** — jurisdiction up to $15,000 in civil matters, misdemeanors, small claims, evictions, and preliminary hearings (NRS Chapter 4).
- **Municipal Courts** — city-level criminal courts for municipal ordinance violations (NRS Chapter 5).
- **Federal**: U.S. District Court for the District of Nevada (Reno, Las Vegas); appeals to the Ninth Circuit.

## 4. Procedural Rules

- **Nevada Rules of Civil Procedure (NRCP)** — mirror many federal rules but with Nevada-specific timing (e.g., NRCP 12 motions, NRCP 26 discovery, NRCP 56 summary judgment).
- **Nevada Rules of Appellate Procedure (NRAP)** — including NRAP 3, 4 (notice of appeal), NRAP 17 (deflection to Court of Appeals).
- **Nevada Rules of Professional Conduct (RPC)** — mirror ABA Model Rules with Nevada variations; administered by the State Bar of Nevada.
- **Eighth Judicial District Court Rules (EDCR)** and other district-specific rules supplement NRCP.
- **Justice Court Rules of Civil Procedure (JCRCP)** — simplified procedure for Justice Court matters.

## 5. Citation Conventions

- **Statutes**: "NRS 78.138" (chapter.section). Use "NRS" not "N.R.S.".
- **Administrative Code**: "NAC 463.150".
- **Court Rules**: "NRCP 12(b)(5)", "NRAP 4(a)", "RPC 1.7", "EDCR 2.20".
- **Cases**: prefer Nevada Reports ("138 Nev. 1, 503 P.3d 1097 (2022)") when available; otherwise parallel cite to Pacific Reporter. Post-2014 Nevada Supreme Court opinions issued as "___ Nev. ___" pending official publication.
- **Court of Appeals**: "Nev. Ct. App." parenthetical.
- **Unpublished**: per NRAP 36(c) unpublished orders are generally not citable except as permitted.

## 6. Common Nevada-Specific Gotchas

- **Community-property state**: spousal property, debt, and divorce analysis differs materially from common-law states (NRS 123).
- **One-action rule**: NRS 40.430 limits creditor remedies on real-property-secured debt to a single action; failure to comply can waive the deficiency or the lien.
- **Anti-deficiency**: NRS 40.451–.459 limits deficiency judgments on residential foreclosures.
- **Non-competes for hourly workers**: prohibited; NRS 613.195(3).
- **Mandatory blue-pencil**: courts MUST modify an overbroad non-compete to make it reasonable; not discretionary.
- **HOA resale package**: 5-day rescission period under NRS 116.4109; failure to deliver can give rise to rescission rights.
- **Gaming licensure**: NRS 463 imposes broad fitness/suitability requirements that can reach lenders, landlords, and creditors of gaming licensees.
- **Commerce Tax threshold**: Nevada Commerce Tax (NRS 363C) applies to businesses with Nevada-sourced gross revenue exceeding $4M annually.
- **No state income tax**: but Modified Business Tax (NRS 363B) on payroll above threshold applies to most employers.
- **Uniform Trust Code variant**: Nevada has a permissive trust regime (NRS 163); often chosen for asset-protection trusts under NRS 166.
